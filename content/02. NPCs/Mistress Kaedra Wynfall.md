*Guild Envoy and Trade Minister; Lady of the Port Elaren; Matron of the Pearl Veil Consortium*
### Quick Facts
**Birthday:** (Age late-30s)
**Appearance:** Half-elf. Lithe and commanding, with bronzed skin weathered just enough to hint at years spent at sea. Her left ear bears three small gold hoops, a tradition among Elaren captains, and she wears a signet of the Pearl Veil Consortium on her right thumb. Her smile is quick, her silences deliberate.
**Disposition:** Suave, opportunistic, magnetic.
**Current Seat:** The Port of Elaren.
### About

Graceful as the tides she governs, Kaedra Wynfall commands the ports of Aurenne with the ease of one born to shifting currents. Her amber eyes glint like sunlit rum over dark seas, and her sea-black curls spill freely to her shoulders, rarely bound, as if in quiet defiance of formality. Always perfumed faintly with salt and exotic spice, she carries the scent of open harbors and distant coasts wherever she walks. Her gowns are cut for freedom of movement and adorned with pearlwork or coral inlays, a subtle display of her maritime wealth.

As Matron of the Pearl Veil Consortium, a loose but powerful alliance of merchant unions, shipwrights, and trade captains, Kaedra’s influence stretches far beyond the docks. She speaks with a sailor’s cadence and a diplomat’s charm, navigating both parliament and pirate courts with effortless poise. Rumors persist of clandestine dealings with corsair lords and the spice cartels of the western isles, yet her mastery of negotiation keeps Aurenne’s fleets prosperous and her rivals placated.
