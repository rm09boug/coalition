### Quick Facts
**Birthday:** The 11th of Verdanspire, 621 (Age 21)
**Appearance:** 5’6” with a soft build. Has her mother’s dark, curly hair and her father’s grey-green eyes and pale countenance. Sunken, heavily lidded eyes and a hawkish nose.
**Disposition:** Extroverted, curious. Polite but arrogant. 
**Current Seat:** Princess of [[Aurenne]], betrothed to [[Prince Erasyl Kadin]], a Merchant Prince from [[The Free Cities of Selvarra]]
### Pre-Game History
- Born 621, first child of [[King Roquint Casteyr ]]and [[Seriane of Deyrholm]], his consort.
- In 625, [[Prince Corvin Casteyr]] is born. The first son of the King, and child of [[Queen Virelle Casteyr]], he becomes the heir apparent.
- In 627, Queen Virelle gives birth to a daughter, Alerienne. She is frail, and dies only two months later.
	- The relationship between Virelle and the young Esra begins to sour.
- Around 628, [[Mira]] arrives as ward to the King.
- Queen Virelle dies of a fever in 631. Corvin is 8, and Esra is 12. The two are very close.
	- [[Sir Renault Durand]] becomes Esra's sworn knight.
- Roquint remarries in 632, and [[Queen Sylveth Casteyr]] gives birth to twins in 633.
	- Esra and Sylveth are only two years apart in age; they may have even known each other as children.
- At some point, [[Kismet]] is assigned to protect Corvin. Corvin spends more and more time with the King, Esra and the twins do not see their father often.
	- Esra spends a lot of time caring for the twins while their mother does fantasy opium.
- In 641, Esra is betrothed to [[Prince Erasyl Kadin]] of [[The Free Cities of Selvarra]].
	- Esra is accosted in the streets around this time. [[Kismet]] (?) comes to her rescue.
	- [[Sir Renault Durand\|Sir Renault]] is quietly retired.
- Prince Kadin's half-nephew [[Rajendra Darakan]] arrives at court to collect Esra (?)
- Game start :]
