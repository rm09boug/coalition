### Merrowen
_The Drowned Crown_  
Southeast of the Iron Holds, beyond the mountain range, there once stood Merrowen, a vast and fertile realm of rivers, marble causeways, and terraced cities that glittered beneath the mountain’s shadow. Its plains stretched for miles toward what was once open countryside and are now a sunken mire. When the Great Sundering struck, the earth beneath Merrowen fractured; whole valleys tilted and slid beneath the rising brine as the continental shelf collapsed. The sea poured inward through the rift, swallowing towns, temples, and palaces in a single day.

What remains is [[the Drowned Reach]], a sprawling marshland of blackwater channels and ghostly ruins. At low tide, shattered rooftops and toppled arches rise from the murk like bones, and the wind carries the sound of unseen bells from beneath the silt. Traders skirt its borders, dredging relics from its shallows, and sailors claim that the tides there do not follow the moon.
