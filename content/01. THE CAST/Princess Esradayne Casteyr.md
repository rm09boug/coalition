### Quick Facts
**Birthday:** [[04. WORLD/The Calendar of Aurenne\|The 11th of Sunspire, 621]] (Age 21)
**Born in:** Aurenne City, [[03. LOCATIONS/Aurenne]]
**Appearance:** 5’6” with a soft build. Has her mother’s dark, curly hair and her father’s grey-green eyes and pale countenance. Sunken, heavily lidded eyes and a hawkish nose.
**Disposition:** Extroverted, curious. Polite but arrogant. 
**Current Seat:** Princess of [[03. LOCATIONS/Aurenne]], betrothed to [[02. NPCs/Prince Erasyl Kadin]], a Merchant Prince from [[03. LOCATIONS/The Free Cities of Selvarra]]
### Pre-Game History
- Born 621, first child of [[King Roquint Casteyr ]]and [[02. NPCs/Seriane of the East-wind]], his consort.
- In 624, [[02. NPCs/Prince Corvin Casteyr]] is born. The first son of the King, and child of [[02. NPCs/Queen Virelle Casteyr]], he becomes the heir apparent.
- In 627, Queen Virelle gives birth to a daughter, Alerienne. She is frail, and dies only two months later.
	- The relationship between Virelle and the young Esra begins to sour.
- Around 629, [[01. THE CAST/Mira]] arrives as ward to the King.
- Queen Virelle dies of a fever in 633. Corvin is 9, and Esra is 12. The two are very close.
	- [[02. NPCs/Sir Renault Durand]] becomes Esra's sworn knight.
- Roquint remarries in 634, and [[02. NPCs/Queen Sylveth Casteyr]] gives birth to twins later that year.
- At some point, [[01. THE CAST/Kismet]] is assigned to protect Corvin. Corvin spends more and more time with the King, Esra and the twins do not see their father often.
	- Around this time, Esra is taken captive during a visit to the city.
	- She is later rescued by one of Renault's squires. [[02. NPCs/Sir Renault Durand\|Sir Renault]] is quietly retired.
	- The experience causes Esra to sequester herself within the walls of the keep.
- In 640, Esra's mother is killed in a riding accident after spending several months away from the castle. Esra withdraws further.
- In 641, Esra is betrothed to [[02. NPCs/Prince Erasyl Kadin]] of [[03. LOCATIONS/The Free Cities of Selvarra]].
- Prince Kadin's half-nephew [[01. THE CAST/Nasrin Rajat Darakan]] arrives at court to collect Esra (?)
- Game start :]
