*The Elven Riders of the Steppe*

The Rhunari roam Aurenne's southern plains, a vast sweep of amber grass, cold rivers, and rocky hills. To settled peoples, the Rhunari are seen as wild, proud, and impossible to govern. To the elves of forests and high courts, they are an ancient cousin-line that never bent the knee to civilization. Storms roll across the plains like stampedes, and the Rhunari ride with them.
### The Children of the Four Winds
The Rhunari are lean, wind-burnt elves that are stockier than their other elven cousins. Their skin tones range from bronzed tan to cool olive; their eyes often amber, grey-green, or near black and their hair coal, sable, or earth brown and worn in long braids wrapped with bone beads and woven Khaeni hair. Tattoos of ochre, crimson, or indigo trace across their arms and jawlines, diagrams of their lineage that show which direction their blood has ridden.

They wear layered leathers and felted wool dyed in earthen hues, and don mantles of horn, feather, and hammered bronze to mark clan and calling. Every Rhunari belongs to a wind-clan, named for the direction of wind that guides their migrations.
#### Clan Aurenkha (Hawkwind)
*Guardians of the North Wind*
The Aurenkha are protectors of the great Khaeni herds, claiming lineage from the first elves who broke from the forest and tamed the steppe. They are pragmatic, proud, and somewhat isolationist.
#### Clan Veythar (Dustblooded)
*Warriors of the South Wind*
With a reputation for raiding and some of the most massive Khaeni mounts among all Rhunari, the Veythar are feared by outsiders and kin alike. They are renowned for their ritual duels, where warriors ride in tight circles trading arrow and spear until blood is drawn.
#### Clan Saelvar (Dawnrush)
*Speakers of the East Wind*
The Saelvar are story-tellers and chroniclers that prize freedom and exchange. Not only do they commonly trade with outsiders, but Saelvar youths will often leave their clans to wander - and sometimes even settle among - human towns for a season.
#### Clan Rhunveil (Cloudstrider)
*Shamans of the West Wind*
Rhunveil shamans are entrusted with the maintenance of the steppe's Waystones and with overseeing the *Tahl Moot*, a grand gathering of the clans held every fourth year to exchange goods, arrange unions, and settle feuds. They tend semi-permanent encampments, where they drape their tents in woven grass streamers that hum in the breeze like haunting birdsong. 
### The Khaeni - Horned Steeds of the Storm
Khaeni (singular Khaen) are massive, short-furred equine beasts, often mistaken for unicorns by outsiders - though the Rhunari would say that’s akin to mistaking a cougar for a house cat.

Khaeni have broad, muscular frames like a warhorse. Each bears a single forward-curving horn, ridged like polished ivory. The horn grows continuously and can be carved with shallow sigils marking its rider’s lineage or victories. Their hides are thick and smooth, colored in shades of gray, dun, or black, sometimes with faint marbling patterns like wind-swept sand. They have storm-grey, pupilless eyes that are as intelligent as they are luminous; Khaeni are said to recognize kin even generations apart.

Khaeni are not domesticated so much as they are befriended through sheer endurance. A Rhunari youth earns adulthood by tracking and running beside a Khaeni herd until a beast accepts their pace. They are fiercely loyal and will not accept another rider unless their bonded partner dies.

Khaeni hooves spark faint arcs of blue-white fire when galloping at full speed, a phenomenon known as *stormlight*. It is considered ill-luck to "ride on stormlight" on a cloudless day.
