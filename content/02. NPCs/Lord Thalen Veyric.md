*High Chancellor of the Treasury; Warden of the Silver Vale*
### Quick Facts
**Birthday:** (Age Mid-40s)
**Appearance:** Human. Tall and solidly built; carries himself with immaculate posture. Wears tailored doublets of muted grey and silver, often adorned with a signet of the Silver Vale. His hands are ink-stained but steady, his gaze unreadable behind rimmed spectacles worn only for close work.
**Disposition:** Pragmatic, discreet, quietly ambitious.
**Current Seat:** The Silver Vale
### About

A broad-shouldered man of middle years, Lord Thalen Veyric dresses with the restraint of a banker and the dignity of a noble. His silver-blond hair is tied neatly at the nape, his beard trimmed close to a single line along his jaw, revealing a face that seems forever deep in thought. Pale eyes, cold, precise, and unblinking, give him the air of one who sees both numbers and men as assets to be balanced. Once a scholar of trade law, now the realm’s master of coin, Thalen rebuilt Aurenne’s mint and stabilized its treasury after years of deficit. Admired for his efficiency yet distrusted for his quiet favoritism toward merchant houses, he remains indispensable to the Crown. To Thalen, prosperity is the truest form of loyalty.
