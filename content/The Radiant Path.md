*The Dawn, the Zenith, and the Eclipse*
### Aurion, the Dawnfather
**Domain:** Renewal, mercy, and the first light of creation
**Symbol:** A golden sun rising over silver waves
**Aspect:** The benevolent face of the Path, patron of healers, judges, and just rulers. Aurion represents hope’s renewal each morning, light reborn from shadow. His temples open at first light, and his priests often bless coronations, peace treaties, and harvests.
**Doctrine:** “The light that forgives also mends.” Aurion teaches compassion, but warns that mercy must not be blind. His worship emphasizes restorative justice and the duty of those with power to shelter the weak.

### Serathiel, the Keeper of the Balance
**Domain:** Judgment, destiny, and the measure between light and dark
**Symbol:** A pair of open hands, one golden, one black, cradling a white flame
**Aspect:** Serathiel embodies the impartial flame of truth, unbent by passion or malice. Neither forgiving nor condemning, they weigh the souls of the dead and the deeds of the living. Their temples are austere courts where both nobles and beggars may seek arbitration.
**Doctrine:** “All light casts a shadow; all shadow defines the light.” Followers of Serathiel believe balance is divine law, that excess of virtue or vice alike leads to ruin.

### Kaerath, the Duskwarden 
**Domain:** Ambition, secrecy, and the transformative power of ruin
**Symbol:** A broken halo burning with red light
**Aspect:** Once the Dawnfather’s most radiant herald, Kaerath’s hunger for creation’s flame outgrew the bounds of mercy and balance. He sought to command light itself, to wield it as a weapon rather than a gift. In his defiance, he rose against Aurion and Serathiel, declaring that only through destruction could perfection be achieved. The two joined their strength to cast him from the heavens, binding him to the [[Nine Hells]] and reshaping his divine form into a monstrous reflection of his ambition, a beast of fire and shadow whose wings drip molten light and whose voice still tempts mortals with promises of greatness.
**Doctrine:** “Only through the fire of want do we learn what must endure.” Kaerath’s followers claim that ruin is the crucible of divinity — that through the purging flame of failure and desire, light is reborn purer and stronger. Though his worship is forbidden in Aurenne, whispers of the [[Duskwarden’s Trial]] persist among assassins, inquisitors, and zealots who believe corruption is not a sin, but a necessary cleansing of a world grown stagnant.
