As dawn illuminated Aurenne City, [[01. THE CAST/Princess Esradayne Casteyr]] was roused by her attendants, with [[Lady Giselda]] leading the way. The elderly matron opened the windows, filling the chamber with morning light, and began dressing Esradayne, who eagerly requested to comb her own hair, a request Lady Giselda reluctantly granted. Shortly after, [[02. NPCs/King Roquint Casteyr\|King Roquint]] entered with two steaming cups of Nekinese coffee, greeting his daughter with a kiss on the forehead, though his nervous demeanor hinted at the significant events ahead.

King Roquint informed Esradayne that she and her siblings were to appear before Parliament to formalize her betrothal to [[02. NPCs/Prince Erasyl Kadin]] of [[03. LOCATIONS/The Free Cities of Selvarra\|Selvarra]], alongside Crown Prince [[02. NPCs/Prince Corvin Casteyr\|Corvin]] and [[01. THE CAST/Mira]]’s own announcement. Concerned for Corvin, Esradayne inquired about his whereabouts, prompting her father to suggest she wake him. He cautioned her about [[02. NPCs/Mistress Kaedra Wynfall]], the guild envoy and trade minister, who might scrutinize their family during the announcements.

After King Roquint departed, Lady Giselda and the attendants finished dressing Esradayne. As she walked towards Corvin’s room, she noticed the disarray of her sister Lucenne's room and overheard a heated argument between [[02. NPCs/Adraston and Lucenne Casteyr]] regarding Lucenne's exclusion from Parliament. Continuing on, she passed Corvin’s Rookery, hearing the familiar sounds of his corvids. Upon entering Corvin’s room, she found him asleep at his desk, drooling beside a half-finished birdcage. A playful tickle with a rookery feather startled him awake, and he expressed more concern for Esradayne’s departure than his own future. Meanwhile, Mira prepared for the day in her private wing, guided by [[02. NPCs/Lady Selena Vaurelle]], who warned her about Mistress Windfall’s probing questions.

After a quick breakfast, Mira rushed across the palace and collided with Esradayne outside Corvin’s door. They shared soft laughter and reassurances before Corvin joined them, looking finery yet tousled from sleep. In the Citadel, [[01. THE CAST/Kismet]], a [[04. WORLD/Crown Keepers]] knight, donned his armor as [[Knight-Captain Ruin]] briefed him on the day's potential recklessness. After receiving a muffin and warm milk from Hazel, the palace baker, Kismet made his way to the children’s wing to meet Esradayne, Mira, and Corvin, confirming their schedules.

The group proceeded to the royal gardens for breakfast, where light conversation flowed in the serene atmosphere. Meanwhile, [[01. THE CAST/Nasrin Rajat Darakan]] and [[01. THE CAST/Captain Vesper Lyell]] navigated the [[04. WORLD/House Lyell\|Cerberus]] towards Argent Bay, with Vesper offering ginger candy to a seasick Nasrin. Spotting the stolen ship [[Polaris]] raised tensions as they approached their destination. Upon arriving at the dignitary docks, Nasrin and Vesper disembarked and were greeted by Esradayne, Mira, and Corvin. Nasrin presented Esradayne with a gift and introduced Vesper, who lightened the formalities with humor.

As gifts were exchanged—Mira receiving elegant ruby-colored gloves and Esradayne a teal hooded capelet—the group prepared to depart for Parliament, the weight of the day’s announcements pressing upon them. With introductions complete and gifts in hand, they turned from the dock, ready to face the expectations and scrutiny awaiting them in the heart of the city.
