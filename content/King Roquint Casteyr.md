*Sovereign of the Realm, Keeper of the Crowned Concord, and High Arbiter of the Veiled Seat*
### Quick Facts
**Birthday:** 582 (Age 60)
**Appearance:** Tall, square-shouldered, with a bird-like nose and short, mousy brown hair that's gone grey.
**Disposition:** Dutiful, reserved, and strategically cautious. Respected as a stabilizer rather than a visionary or reformer.
**Current Seat:** His Majesty the King of Aurenne, Sovereign of the Realm, Keeper of the Crowned Concord, and High Arbiter of the Veiled Seat
### About
Roquint is widely regarded as a competent and steady ruler who has preserved balance more than reshaped it. He is respected by the nobility but not revered, remembered for preventing decline rather than creating golden eras. His relationship with Esra has grown increasingly formal with time; more political than paternal. He trusts her discipline but not her unpredictability - two her lady mother's traits.
