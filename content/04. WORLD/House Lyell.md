The Lyell family is a noble house based out of [[03. LOCATIONS/Aurenne]], born from piracy and now functioning as crown endorsed corsairs along the [[03. LOCATIONS/The Sapphire Coast]]. The family is new to nobility, the line only having been around for 3 generations, starting with Captain Vander Lyell. 

**Vander Lyell** began as a pirate, working his way up the chain of command to become one of the most shrewd and infamous captains on the Sapphire Coast. He made a fortune from the plundering of trade and cargo ships in and out of Aurenne, as well as causing the destruction of a not insignificant number of navel ships. Eventually he was offered a truce by the King. In exchange for his compliance, he would be gifted land, wealth, and a title. The terms were simple: work under the crown as a corsair and bring order to the Sapphire Coast. 

Perhaps some pirates would have seen this as a breach of “code”, or would refuse to serve under any master, but Vander saw this as a golden opportunity and turned his cutthroats into a company of privateers. A couple other captains joined under his command, effectively becoming the beginnings of the Lyell fleet– the [[Azurefang Vanguard]]– and would continue working with the family for the years to come. Vander hunted his rivals, as well as enemies of Aurenne, seized their ships (if not sunk), and gifted the spoils to the crown as a show of loyalty. They were also put in charge of snuffing out smuggling into the land. 

The man knew pirate tactic well, and as such was able to secure many decisive victories with fewer collateral losses than the navy. With the coast beginning to be brought to heel once more, Vander was conferred nobility for his exceptional service. 

He married a commoner and had a son at 30, [[02. CHARACTERS/Admiral Ventus Lyell\|Ventus Lyell]]. He sailed until he was 55, maintained command of the family and his fleet until he was 60, choosing then to retire and pass command to Ventus. Vander died five years later. 

Ventus never became a sailor, instead working as a commander to instruct the fleet from land while focusing on consolidating wealth, enforcing order, and proving the families right to their station. 
#### Public views of the Lyells:
- Feared on the seas as ruthless enforcers. 
- Admired and appreciated by sailors aboard legal vessels for their skill and protection. 
- Resented by nobility, viewed as unruly upstarts who bought their title with blood money. 
- Distrusted by merchants, who due to rumor consider them pirates still; they could be protectors one day and raiders the next
