*Guildmaster of the Iron Standard; Warden of the Foundries*
### Quick Facts
**Birthday:** (Age Mid-50s)
**Appearance:** A mountain of a man, broad-chested and scarred from decades at the forge. His skin bears a permanent bronze hue from heat and smoke, his beard a thick plume of iron-grey threaded with ash. His eyes are a burnished amber, steady, keen, and full of quiet defiance. Often seen in a heavy smith’s apron even when summoned to council, his hammer-signet, “Oathfire,” hangs from a chain around his neck, symbol of the Guild’s founding vow.
**Disposition:** Stoic, principled, fiercely loyal to his craftsmen; blunt to a fault.
**Current Seat:** The Iron Quarter, [[03. LOCATIONS/Aurenne City]].
### About

Jorran Feld is the unyielding heart of Aurenne’s industrial might, a man of soot-stained hands and ironbound principles who rose from the forges of the Foundry Quarter to command the most powerful labor guild in the realm. Where nobles speak in councils and merchants barter over ledgers, Jorran speaks in sparks and steel. His booming voice carries across the furnaces as easily as through Parliament’s echoing halls, where he stands as the living emblem of Industrial Mastery and Labor Honor.

As Guildmaster of the Iron Standard, Jorran oversees the blacksmiths, machinists, foundry-workers, and builders who keep Aurenne’s war engines turning and its city growing. He preaches craftsmanship as creed, that honest work tempers the soul as fire tempers metal. Though loyal to the Crown, he does not kneel easily; he has faced nobles, ministers, and merchants alike with the same steady glare of a man who cannot be bought. To the workers, he is mentor and defender. To his rivals, a blunt and immovable obstacle.
