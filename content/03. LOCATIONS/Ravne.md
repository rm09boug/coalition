Ravne is a hereditary monarchy unlike any other—a nation ruled not merely by crown or conquest, but by the sacred geometry of blood. At its core lies the Founder's Line, a tightly-guarded ancestral thread that stretches back to a near-mythic progenitor whose name is etched in every noble tongue and legal document. Rule is not passed through primogeniture or merit, but awarded to the one who can trace the shortest, purest path to that founder—often resulting in a throne passed not to a firstborn, but to a favored cousin, uncle, or niece. The royal family, the House of Ravne, has woven itself into the fabric of the entire kingdom through generations of strategic intermarriage, creating a vast, thorny web of relation that spans every duchy, manor, and provincial court.

Each duchy is governed by a bloodbearing relative of the ruling line—never strangers, never outsiders. Every governor is kin, bound by oaths, politics, and occasionally marriage, to the sovereign seated in [[Ravnehold]], the capital palace-city nestled in the ancestral heartland. The nobles of Ravne are obsessed with lineage, and great halls are hung not with battle standards, but with sprawling genealogical tapestries. Names are sacred, marriages arranged by mathematicians as much as matchmakers, and no bond is stronger than the blood that binds. Ravne is stable, insular, and quietly ruthless—a nation that will never be conquered from without, because it has already conquered itself from within.

[[Highfather Vaelric the Firstborn]], the kingdom's founder, is said to have carved Ravne from the bones of warring tribes. His blood is the standard; all claimants trace back to him.

### Great Houses of Ravne
#### House Caelveth
House Caelveth is the ruling branch of Ravne—an elite bloodline that has sat upon the throne for eleven generations, tracing a direct, unbroken path back to Highfather Vaelric the Firstborn through only five branches, the fewest of any known noble house. They are widely considered the purest blood in the kingdom, and their genealogical tree is preserved in the Living Wall of Ravnehold: a tapestry stitched from enchanted silk and bloodthread that updates magically with every Caelveth birth and death. [[02. CHARACTERS/Queen Ysvara Caelveth]] is the head of this house and has ruled Ravne for the last 17 years.

*"Blood is the root. All else is ornament."*
**House Sigil:** A coiled crimson serpent wrapped around a black tree root, set against a bone-white field.
**House Colors:** Blood red, bone white, and blackroot gray
#### House Virellan
Known as the “Twelfth Branch,” House Virellan is a cadet line with only slightly longer genealogical distance from the Founder than the ruling House Ravne. Though officially loyal, they are whispered to have “purer hearts than blood” and house many intellectuals, generals, and reform-minded nobles.
