*Gateway of Diplomacy*

A sunlit southern coastline dotted with embassies, villas, and sea fortresses. Known for its azure bays and vineyards, [[03. LOCATIONS/The Sapphire Coast]] is Aurenne’s window to the world  - where envoys, spies, and merchant princes weave alliances under banners of courtesy and wine-stained smiles.
### Cities

#### Iskara
*The City on the Strait*
Half on the mainland and half upon the great Isle of Karr, Iskara stretches across the strait that bears its name, joined by a vast stone bridge and chains of harbor lights. The mainland quarter handles commerce and diplomacy; the island quarter houses gardens, galleries, and the famed Collegium of Astronomy.  It's culture is polished and cosmopolitan, with a hint of rivalry between “mainlanders” and “islanders.”
#### Virelda
*City of Wine and Warfare*
Perched on a high, sun-drenched cliff on Aurenne's southern coast, Virelda is looks out over the glittering {} Sea. Once a naval stronghold, its bastions now overlook terraces of vines and olive groves. The city is renowned for its wines and its academy of navigation, where nobles and commoners alike learn the art of command at sea.
