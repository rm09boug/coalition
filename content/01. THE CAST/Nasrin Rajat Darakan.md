*Second son of [[04. WORLD/House Darakan]]; Lord of Ramona’s Mirror; Administrator of Maharasa Syndicate*
### Quick Facts
**Birthday:** (Age 23)
**Born in:** [[03. LOCATIONS/The Deyrholm Frontier]]
**Appearance:** Tall and athletic. His skin holds a rich, sun-deepened brown, as though the daylight favors him. His eyes are a striking blue, matching neither parent. His hair is trimmed short, dyed a light tan that fades into his natural chestnut brown.
**Current Seat:** Lord of Ramona’s Mirror
### Pre-Game History
Darakan upbringing is a boot camp wrapped in luxury blankets. If one is to bear the name of the clan, they are not expected to meet expectations, but to surpass them. Nasrin was no exception, and his early life mirrored that of countless Darakan sons and daughters who came before him. He underwent strenuous studies and martial training more than he was able to enjoy being a child. Each year, a tap to the marble that shaped him into nobility worthy of his ancestry.

Unlike his elder brother, Avalin, Nasrin took to his lessons quickly. Rather than resent the droning retelling of battles fought and won, he warmed to them, idolising the men and women of the tales. Nasrin, too, wished to be a story to be told to a generation of children.

At the time of writing, Nasrin was married to [[01. THE CAST/Captain Vesper Lyell]], first son of [[04. WORLD/House Lyell]], for roughly three months. Though he does not think ill of the arrangement, he also does not feel particularly attached to his new life partner, either. He considers it a worthy price for the benefit of his family, and nothing more. The agreement set between both families are, to his knowledge, for unlocking trade routes on the seas without counting as many coin to cover fees.
