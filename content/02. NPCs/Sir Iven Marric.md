*Master of the Civic Guard; Captain of the Capital Watch; Standard-Bearer of the Stoneward Brotherhood*
### Quick Facts
**Birthday:** (Age late-90s)
**Appearance:** Stocky and powerfully built, with corded arms and a weathered face often split by a wry grin. His armor bears the scuffs of constant use, though the polished bronze crest of the Civic Guard gleams proudly upon his breastplate. Wears a dark blue cloak lined with steel thread and carries a short-bladed halberd nicknamed Crowbreaker.
**Disposition:** Stalwart, outspoken, loyal to the people but wary of politics.
**Current Seat:** The Capital Guard, [[03. LOCATIONS/Aurenne City]].
### About

Compact, broad-shouldered, and carved from the same grit as Aurenne’s streets, Sir Iven Marric stands as the people’s knight, a dwarf who rose from the city guard’s ranks to command its entire force. His beard, thick and iron-black, is streaked with grey and neatly bound in silver clasps, while his dark eyes gleam with equal measures of humor and defiance. The heavy set of his jaw hints at stubborn endurance, the kind that neither politics nor privilege can erode.

Once a dockhand turned guardsman, Iven earned his knighthood through years of hard service and unflinching loyalty during the Riots of the Silver Quarter. Beloved by commonfolk for his fairness and visible presence among them, he is nonetheless distrusted by Aurenne’s highborn peers for his populist sympathies and refusal to bow too deeply to privilege. As Standard-Bearer of the Stoneward Brotherhood — an informal union of dwarven craftsmen, civic guards, and labor wardens — he ensures that the working heart of the capital remains both protected and heard.
