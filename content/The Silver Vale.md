*Breadbasket and Coinhold of Aurenne*

Rolling plains and river-vales rich with farmland and silver-veined hills. [[The Silver Vale]] is famed for their minted coinage, fine horses, and merchant nobles. Pragmatic and profit-driven, their allegiance often follows the weight of gold more than the pull of honor.
### Cities

#### Norcliff
*The Northern Gateway*
On the northern coast, where the [[River Selmarch]] meets the [[Boreal Sea]], Norcliff stands as the Vale’s northern harbor and its link to the colder, rougher coasts beyond. The city’s high granite walls gleam faintly with embedded ore, and its wharves are crowded with merchant vessels bound inland. Known for shipwrights, fur traders, and the unflinching temperament of its people, Norcliff is weathered, loyal to its contracts, and proud of its endurance.
#### Eryndross
_The Timber Shore_  
East of Norcliff, where the Boreal winds sweep through slate forests and salt-crusted cliffs, lies Eryndross, a city built upon the bones of ancient groves. Here grow the [[Aelvryn Oaks]], vast and luminous trees whose heartwood shimmers faintly with silver veins of latent magic. Harvesting them is both craft and ritual; each felling is overseen by woodwardens who bind the tree’s spirit before the first cut is made.

The timber of Eryndross is prized across Aurenne, flexible as steel, unyielding to rot, and faintly resonant with enchantment. It shapes the masts of royal ships, the beams of keeps, and the frames of spell-forges alike. The city itself clings to the cliffs in terraces of wood and stone, its mills driven by the tide and its air heavy with the mingled scent of resin and brine. Its people are as enduring as the trees they hew: solemn, disciplined, and ever mindful that every plank they shape once stood whispering to the stars.
#### Lunethar
_The Mist at the Summit_  
At the crown of [[Fog Lake]], Lunethar rises like a reflection of the mist itself, its terraces of pale stone mirrored in the still waters below. The city began as a mining post and ferry haven, but grew into the Vale’s inland retreat for merchant lords seeking cool air and calm. The morning fog cloaks its marble villas and lakeside quays, earning it the name “the city that dreams.” Beneath its serenity lies quiet industry: silver ingots, polished and sealed in wax, leave its foundries bound for the mints of [[Aurenne's Heartland#Valmere\|Valemere]] and [[Aurenne City]].

Lunethar’s people are patient and refined, valuing discretion as much as diligence. The lake’s fog, they say, hides both sins and fortunes, and the calm waters never give up secrets.
#### Veyrun’s Gate
_Where Waters Meet_  
Downstream from the [[Calven Run]], where its current merges with the [[River Veyrun]] flowing from [[The Deyrholm Frontier]], stands Veyrun’s Gate. The river’s convergence carves deep channels through the slate ridges, creating natural harbors for barges descending from Fog Lake and caravans arriving from the frontier passes.

The Gate itself straddles the narrowest point of the Veyrun, fortified with stone arches and watchtowers whose lanterns burn day and night. Coin flows like water here, taxes, tariffs, and tolls enriching both crown and merchant alike. Its people are sharp-eyed, sharp-tongued, and ever-listening to the river’s voice; they say it murmurs the worth of every soul who dares to cross.
#### Twinriver
_The Crossroads of the Heartlands_  
Where the Rivers Velyn and Arros converge and meander toward [[The Vale of Rhaenne]], Twinriver straddles their floodplains, a city of bridges, mills, and ceaseless motion. It began as a crossing for farmers and merchants, then swelled into a vital trade nexus linking Aurenne’s fertile [[Aurenne's Heartland\|Heartlands]] to the Silver Vale’s markets. Its twin bridges, the First Span and the Last Span, mark the border where grain meets silver.

Twinriver thrives on negotiation; every street corner hosts a deal, every tavern a contract. Its people are clever, hospitable, and rarely idle. Travelers say the city itself seems to speak, the creak of wagons, the clatter of hooves, and the hum of coin echoing between its two rivers like the pulse of the realm.
