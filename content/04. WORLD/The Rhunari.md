*The Elven Riders of the Deyrholm Frontier*

The Rhunari roam the Deyrholm Frontier, a vast sweep of amber grass, cold rivers, and rocky hills. To settled peoples, the Rhunari are seen as wild, proud, and impossible to govern. To the elves of forests and high courts, they are an ancient cousin-line that never bent the knee to civilization. Storms roll across the plains like stampedes, and the Rhunari ride with them.
### The Khaeni - Horned Steeds of the Frontier
Khaeni (singular Khaen) are massive, short-furred equine beasts, often mistaken for unicorns by outsiders - though the Rhunari would say that’s like mistaking a cougar for a house cat.

**Build:** Broad, muscular frames like a warhorse.
**Horn:** Each bears a single forward-curving horn, ridged like polished ivory. The horn grows continuously and can be carved with shallow sigils marking its rider’s lineage or victories.
**Hide:** Their hides are thick and smooth, colored in shades of gray, dun, or black, sometimes with faint marbling patterns like wind-swept sand.
**Eyes:** Intelligent and luminous; Khaeni are said to recognize kin even generations apart.

Khaeni are not domesticated so much as befriended through endurance. A Rhunari youth earns adulthood by tracking and running beside a Khaeni herd until a beast accepts their pace. They are fiercely loyal and will not accept another rider unless their bonded partner dies.

Their hooves spark faint arcs of blue-white fire when galloping at full speed, a phenomenon known as *stormlight*.
