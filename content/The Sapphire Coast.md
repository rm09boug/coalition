*Gateway of Diplomacy*

A sunlit southern coastline dotted with embassies, villas, and sea fortresses. Known for its azure bays and vineyards, [[The Sapphire Coast]] is Aurenne’s window to the world  - where envoys, spies, and merchant princes weave alliances under banners of courtesy and wine-stained smiles.
### Cities

#### Iskara
*The City on the Strait*
Half on the mainland and half upon the great Isle of Karr, Iskara stretches across the strait that bears its name, joined by a vast stone bridge and chains of harbor lights. The mainland quarter handles commerce and diplomacy; the island quarter houses gardens, galleries, and the famed Collegium of Astronomy.  It's culture is polished and cosmopolitan, with a hint of rivalry between “mainlanders” and “islanders.”
#### Port Merinth
*The Iron Artery*
Where the River Soren meets the sea, Port Merinth sprawls across lush deltas rich with reeds and salt fields. Barges from [[The Iron Holds]] arrive daily, and great chain-cranes haul ore and armaments from the river to the sea docks, where trade ships bound for [[Port Elaren]] wait. The city’s air hums with the rhythm of labor: waterwheels, windmills, and market bells.
#### Terrin's Reach
*The Last Light of the West*
At the far southwestern edge of the continent, Terrin’s Reach clings to red cliffs battered by surf. Small and windswept, it began as a watch-fort and grew into a haven for pearl-divers and exiles, each with supertitions more outrageous than the last. Its lighthouse, Aurion's Eye, is the last beacon Aurenne’s ships see before open ocean.
#### Virelda
*City of Wine and Warfare*
Perched on a high, sun-drench cliff south of Port Elaren, Virelda is surrounded by glittering seas. Once a naval stronghold, its bastions now overlook terraces of vines and olive groves. The city is renowned for its wines and its academy of navigation, where nobles and commoners alike learn the art of command at sea.
