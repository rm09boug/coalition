*Minister of Defense; Commander of the Iron Holds*
### Quick Facts
**Birthday:** (Age early-50s)
**Appearance:** Broad and weathered, with arms corded by years of swordwork and armor weight. His eyes are a deep, iron-blue, hard and steady beneath a furrowed brow. Prefers plain military garb even in council, his cloak pinned by a simple iron clasp bearing the crest of the Holds, a mountain cleft by a sword. His left hand shows signs of an old frostbite, two fingers stiff and pale.
**Disposition:** Gruff, steadfast, distrustful of diplomacy yet loyal beyond reproach.
**Current Seat:** The Iron Holds.
### About

A rugged veteran with a soldier’s poise and a commander’s glare, Lord Fenric Thassel bears the weight of decades spent on Aurenne’s bloodied borders. His face is lined and battleworn, a living chronicle of the Border Wars that forged his reputation. Coarse iron-grey hair falls to his shoulders, often tied back with a strip of leather, and a jagged scar runs from temple to jaw, a relic from the Siege of Coldmarch. His voice carries the clipped authority of a man long accustomed to command, and his presence alone can silence an entire war council.

Fenric commands the Iron Holds, the fortress chain guarding Aurenne’s southeastern passes, where loyalty is measured in survival and the line between soldier and civilian blurs. Though elevated to Minister of Defense, he remains a soldier at heart, suspicious of diplomacy, wary of foreign aid, and loyal only to the steel that keeps the realm secure. To his men, he is both hammer and shield; to his peers, an unmovable obstacle.
