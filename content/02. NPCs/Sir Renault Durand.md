*Former Crownsguard of Aurenne; Tolerance; Knight of the Silver Reins*
### Quick Facts
**Age:** Mid-50s
**Appearance:** Broad-shouldered, clean-shaven save for a thick grey moustache; weathered skin and steady blue eyes. Moves with a deliberate limp.
**Disposition:** Stoic, disciplined, quietly haunted.
**Current Seat:** Durand Manor, on the edge of the royal vineyards of Aurenne.
### About
Once the steadfast shield of [[02. NPCs/Queen Virelle Casteyr]], and later that of [[01. THE CAST/Princess Esradayne Casteyr]], Rhenault Durand now walks the slower paths of age and regret. In his youth, he was famed across Aurenne for his horsemanship and unshakable poise in battle, a knight whose blade struck true and whose steeds seemed born of his own spirit. Silver now streaks his once-black hair, and the old wound in his left leg lends him a slight limp that time has turned into a mark of endurance rather than defeat.

After the capture of the young Princess under his watch, Rhenault was quietly retired, rewarded with a manor, vineyard, and horse ranch in the western reaches of Aurenne, courtesy of the Crown. There he tends to his bloodline of warhorses and bottles of wine with equal care, nursing both his injuries and his conscience. Though no longer of the Crownsguard, his counsel is still sought by those who remember his discipline and quiet wisdom.

To [[01. THE CAST/Kismet\|Kismet]], his protégé, he is both mentor and friend, a man whose lessons are measured in discipline and precision. Though his past has carved its marks upon him, he carries it with calm acceptance, teaching not through sorrow but through mastery. In his presence, there is steadiness, the quiet certainty of one who has weathered loss and found peace in purpose.

His mentorship is stern and exacting, yet punctuated by moments of unexpected warmth and quiet compassion.
