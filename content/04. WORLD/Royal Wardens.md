**Ideology:** Martial Service / Crowned Order
**Commander:** [[02. NPCs/Marshal Corin Deyrsson]], Lord of [[03. LOCATIONS/The Deyrholm Frontier]]
**Symbol:** A silver sword upright upon a field of black and gold
**Seat of Influence:** The Bulwark, [[03. LOCATIONS/Aurenne City]]
### About
The Royal Wardens stand as the Crown’s mailed fist and the kingdom’s silent shield, a sworn brotherhood of knights who bridge the authority of the nobility and the people. Where the [[04. WORLD/Crown Keepers]] guard the monarch and the [[Civic Guard]] patrols the streets, the Wardens enforce royal law across both, acting as mobile adjudicators, escorts, and military peacekeepers throughout the capital and the provinces.

Each Warden swears an oath known as The Covenant: obedience to the throne, impartiality before the law, and restraint in bloodshed. Trained in both cavalry warfare and urban discipline, they are permitted to cross jurisdictions that other forces cannot, a privilege that earns them both fear and resentment.

The Wardens’ authority is absolute in times of crisis; Parliament must yield to their command when the Crown invokes The Right of Wardenship, a decree allowing them to suspend civil command under threat to stability. This balance of power makes them both indispensable and dangerous, protectors of order who could, in the wrong hands, become its undoing.

Their presence is marked by quiet discipline: black cloaks trimmed in gold, visors polished to a mirror sheen, and swords without ornament, for they believe that “a weapon should reflect only its bearer.”

To the people, they are the Iron Watchers.

## The Sentinels (Subfaction)

**Ideology:** Service / Obedience / Vigilance
**Commander:** Captain-General [[02. NPCs/Sir Joran Thale]]
**Symbol:** A black tower crowned with a silver flame
**Seat of Influence:** The Bulwark, Aurenne City
### About
The Crown Sentinels are the enlisted arm of the Royal Wardens, foot soldiers, riders, and guardsmen drawn from common and provincial bloodlines who uphold the Wardens’ mandates across the realm. While the Wardens themselves act as officers, judges, and knights-errant, the Sentinels are their boots and blades, carrying out arrests, securing roads, and maintaining the royal garrisons scattered across the land.

Unlike the Wardens’ gold-trimmed cloaks, the Sentinels wear plain black mantles with pips denoting their rank. Their helms bear a vertical slit visor, giving them the aspect of statues in the torchlight. Many call them “the Black Flame,” for their discipline and the faint glint of their watch-fires seen along the highways at night.

To serve among the Sentinels is to aspire upward, those who distinguish themselves may be anointed into the Wardens’ ranks, a rare elevation that binds their name to the Covenant.
