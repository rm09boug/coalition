*Voice of the Faith; Keeper of the Radiant Path*
### Quick Facts
**Birthday:** (Age early-50s)
**Appearance:** Elf. Tall and spare, with high cheekbones and silver-threaded hair concealed beneath her veil. Her hands are long and unadorned save for the radiant sigil ring of her office. Moves with a deliberate, graceful austerity that commands silence.
**Disposition:** Stern, devout, incorruptible.
**Current Seat:** The Temple of the Radiant Path, Aurenne City.
### About

Serayne Valenwode carries herself like a living relic, tall, ascetic, and cloaked in the quiet authority of the devout. Her face is narrow and severe, its pallor made almost luminous beneath the white-gold veils of her order. Clear blue eyes hold a piercing calm, the sort that unravels falsehoods before words are even spoken. As a direct descendant of the canonized Saint Tiria Valenwode, she bears both the weight of sanctity and the scrutiny of lineage. Her sermons are disciplined and exacting, her judgments merciless when the faith or moral order of the realm is threatened. Though outwardly serene, her conviction burns with the unrelenting heat of doctrine.
