*Director-General of the Aurentine Bank; Mirror of Coin*
### Quick Facts
**Birthday:** (Age 51)
**Appearance:** Lean, brown-haired, and hawk-eyed; his robes are trimmed with understated gold filigree, a subtle reminder of power earned rather than displayed.
**Disposition:** Cerebral, exacting, and unnervingly calm. Maeric speaks rarely but decisively, with the weight of one who has already calculated the outcome.
**Current Seat:** The Aurentine Vault, [[03. LOCATIONS/Aurenne City]]
### About

Maeric Solvarn presides over the golden heart of Aurenne’s economy, the Aurentine Bank, where wealth, politics, and prophecy converge in quiet arithmetic. Once a scholar of numeromancy at the [[Gilded Lyceum]], his fascination with the hidden language of value turned from magic to money. Solvarn believes coin is the purest form of order, incorruptible when properly accounted for, and under his rule, the Bank became both cathedral and fortress to that creed. Through careful loans, veiled influence, and subtle manipulation of currency, he steadied the realm after the last famine and ensured the coffers bled into every noble purse.
