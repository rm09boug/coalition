### Quick Facts
**Birthday:** The 17th of Ashfall, 618 A.F
**Born in:** [[03. LOCATIONS/Aurenne City]]
**Appearance:** 5' 11", lithe frame and lean muscled. His graphite colored hair touches his collarbone in soft waves, usually seen with the top half pulled up into a small ponytail. His bangs are struck through with white on the right side, though despite its color, his hair retains its luster, suggesting the hue is not the result of aging. Deep set silver-grey eyes, bow-shaped lips often pulled into a perpetual faint smirk. Ears pierced in several places, he is often wearing a multitude of rings and layered necklaces. The cerberus tattoo on his chest is typically proudly on display through open tops.
**Disposition:** Charming, cocky, impulsive, and playful. Sometimes comes across as cold.
**Current Seat:** Eldest son of the Lyell family, Captain of the "[[Cerberus]]", one of the flagships for the corsair fleet. 
### Pre-Game History
- 589: Notorious pirate Captain (and thorn in the royal Navy's side) [[Vander Lyell]] is approached by the crown at 25
	- Vander is asked to end his reign of terror over the coast in exchange for land, and a title, provided he would also serve as a corsair to put a stop to the rampant piracy on the coast
- 590: Vander is appointed as a Lord for his exceptional service to the crown
	- The Lyell name is no longer synonymous with piracy, instead now well known as the one who brought order back to the [[03. LOCATIONS/The Sapphire Coast\|Sapphire Coast]] as ruthless hunters of pirates and inhibitor of illegal importing or smuggling. 
- 593: Vander marries a commoner.
- 594: [[Ventus Lyell]] is born, the only child of Vander.
- 616: Ventus marries [[Aurora Blackwood]], the middle daughter of a winemaking family.
- 618: Vesper is born 
- 621: [[Xander Lyell]] (middle brother) is born
- 624: [[Aster Lyell]] (youngest brother) is born. Vander retires at 60 and passes the legacy on to Ventus (30)
- 625: [[Juniper Lyell]] (sister) is born
- 629: Vander dies at age 65
- 637: Vesper appointed as Captain of the [[Polaris]]
	- During this time, Vesper begins to make a name for himself, known on the sea for his daring but genius tactics in ship combat
- 640: (Ashfall) Vesper's ship, The [[Polaris]], is attacked; there are heavy casualties on both sides. Vesper disappears for 6 months, presumed dead after falling into the icy sea. His body is not recovered, but his family holds a funeral for him all the same. 
- 641: (Goldspire) Vesper reappears in [[03. LOCATIONS/Aurenne City]]
	- By the time he makes his way back to Aurenne City, his family had already since mourned him, assigned Xander as heir. Vesper reclaims his position, to his brother's chagrin.
	- The now repaired Polaris had been reassigned to a different captain. Vesper concedes easily, requesting to have his own ship built. That ship was named [[Cerberus]].
	- Undaunted by the incident, Vesper takes back to the sea as soon as his ship is ready, reclaiming those of his previous crew who still wished to serve under his command. 
- 642: Vesper marries [[01. THE CAST/Nasrin Rajat Darakan]]
	- Vesper arrives alongside Nasrin to collect the [[01. THE CAST/Princess Esradayne Casteyr]], serving as her means of conveyance to her betrothed.
