*Duchess of Ramona’s Mirror; Overseer of the Darakan Estate*
### Quick Facts
**Birthday:** (Age 45)
**Appearance:** Dark-skinned. Mayari can be regularly witnessed in the latest that fashion can offer, preferring material tailored to compliment her full and curvy figure. Her once noticeably youthful features have become refined with the beginnings of her late forties. Long, deep black hair kept high in styles and only let down in the privacy of her chambers in which she shares with her husband.
**Disposition:** Mayari’s kindness has taken many off guard, considering she is the love of such a cold man. The beauty that shapes her is acknowledged and worn by her as finely as any jewelry. She is a woman who enjoys laughing, gossiping with noblewomen, and conversing endlessly over baked treats and tea. She is bold and intelligent, and she shows her love for her family quickly and openly.
**Current Seat:** Ramona's Mirror
### About

Mayari was married into the Darakan family when she was young, having been selected by Orion Darakan for her exceptional education and careful hands when she sat before a piano.

Duke Orion’s partner in crime. Were it not for her, he would be shipping spices in bottle crates. While commerce is delegated to the sons in the household to manage, the Duchess spends most of her time caring for the estate, commanding servants each season to swap the decor accordingly, submitting measurements to tailors regularly to keep up with high fashion’s constant rotation, arranging Galas for nobility friends, and prospecting business partners.
