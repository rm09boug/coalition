*Warden to Lady Chyre Darakan*
### Quick Facts
**Birthday:** (Age 16)
**Appearance:** Thife is a slender teenager with signs of muscle in his arms. He typically wears House Darakan armor, with a special brooch on his cape to signify his status as personal guard and life servant. He has pale hair, brown eyes, and sunbleached skin. 
**Disposition:** Thife doesn’t speak. He can understand common fine and take orders, but the most he can vocalize are grunts when injured. He shows respect for the Darakan family, but keeps his distance. He seems to be warm with Chyre.
**Current Seat:** 
### About

Thife was discovered by [[02. NPCs/Duke Orion Darakan]] in a settlement not too far from [[Ramona’s Mirror]]. The boy was traveling with a mercenary gang, parentless, voiceless, thin and having only a sword in his possession. Seeing the potential and strength in the young soldier, Thife was brought back to the estate, under the condition that he serve as [[02. NPCs/Lady Chyre Darakan]]’s warden for the rest of his days. He will never wed, never sire, and never own land.
