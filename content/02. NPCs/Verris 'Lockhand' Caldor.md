*Guildmaster of the Syndicate*
### Quick Facts
**Birthday:** (Late 40s)
**Appearance:** Lean and sinewy half-orc, with sharp features and an ever-present smirk that never quite reaches his eyes. His dark hair is streaked with silver and tied back in a loose knot, while his right hand, the famed “Lockhand,” is an intricate steel prosthetic of dwarven make, its joints engraved with sigils of protection and silence. Dresses in layered leather coats and shadowed greys, favoring function over flair. His voice is smooth, low, and edged with the cadence of old Aurenne’s streets.
**Disposition:** Cunning, composed, ruthlessly pragmatic; a criminal philosopher.
**Current Seat:** The Vein, [[03. LOCATIONS/Aurenne City]].
### About

Known in whispers and wanted posters more than in public record, Verris Caldor, called “Lockhand” for the steel-bound gauntlet he wears on his right arm, is the unspoken power that moves beneath Aurenne’s city streets. Once a master locksmith and thief-catcher in the Capital Guard, Verris vanished for a decade after a scandal that saw half the city’s vaults compromised. He returned not as a fugitive, but as the Guildmaster of the [[Syndicate]], the clandestine network that controls Aurenne’s smuggling rings, debt markets, and black coin exchanges.

Under his leadership, the Syndicate has evolved from a criminal collective into a shadow bureaucracy, the invisible mirror of the Inherent itself. Verris enforces order through fear and favor alike, turning thieves into informants and rivals into clients. The nobles who condemn him by day often petition his services by night. His intelligence network rivals the Crown’s own, and his loyalty, though for sale, is always delivered with precision.
