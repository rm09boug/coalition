*First Queen of Roquint’s Reign*
### Quick Facts
**Birthday:** 589 (Died age 42 in 631)
**Appearance:** Slender, with warm olive skin and chestnut hair.
**Disposition:** Regal, devout, and unrelentingly proper even when embarrassed or insulted.
**Current Seat:** Deceased
### About
Virelle’s legacy persists strongly in court culture and etiquette, with her exacting standards still shaping expectations of Corvin's success as future king. Virelle tolerated many political threats in her life but never forgave the existence of Seriane; even if she masked it impeccably in public, in private circles she made no attempt to hide her contempt for the king's consort. She saw Esra as a living humiliation and a clear threat to her legacy, but treated her with kindness - tinged with a bit of distrust regarding her closeness to Corvin - until the death of her own daughter. She died of a fever in 631, and was mourned by the kingdom entire. Even in death, Virelle remains a measure that all of the royal house's women are judged against.
