### About

For Orion, the world is divided into two kinds of people: those born to kneel - and himself. His not-so-humble seat as Duke of [[03. LOCATIONS/The Deyrholm Frontier#Ramona's Mirror\|Ramona's Mirror]] is supported by a pedigree that spans generations. The Darakan line has produced fearless lords, royal advisors, and formidable warriors, each rigorously trained in education, trade, politics, history, and culture. Their uncompromising standards have shaped a lineage as prized as the rare imports they command.

Orion, ever mindful of this legacy, considers it only natural that he should shape the court to his will. His ambition extends beyond the present; he envisions a future where the crown rests upon his son’s brow, and he will use whatever - and whoever - is necessary to secure that destiny.
