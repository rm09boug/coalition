*Speaker of the Parliament; Leader of the Circle of the People*
### Quick Facts
**Birthday:** (Age late-30s)
**Appearance:** Human. Of medium height and striking poise, Miren carries herself with the confidence of someone who has fought for every step she’s gained. She has chestnut hair streaked with grey, worn in an elegant twist, and clear hazel eyes that miss little. Her voice is low, precise, and mesmerizing when she speaks. Prefers tailored gowns in deep blues or muted silvers, always marked with a small brooch of the Circle’s crest at her collar.  
**Disposition:** Charismatic, calculating, eloquent. 
**Current Seat:** Aurenne City.
### About

A reformist of uncommon grace and conviction, Lady Miren Solis has risen from merchant-born roots to stand as the voice of Aurenne’s Parliament. Her speeches weave logic with poetry, her words cutting as deftly as any blade on the floor of debate. Through her [[03. LOCATIONS/Aurenne City#Circle of the People\|Circle of the People]] faction, she champions guild rights, fair levies, and the growing voice of the provinces. Though her ideals are noble, her tactics are often subtle, some say manipulative, and her influence within the lower councils is unmatched.
