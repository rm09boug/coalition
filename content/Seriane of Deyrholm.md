*Former Consort of the King*
### Quick Facts
**Birthday:** 570s–580s (appears mid-40s by elven standards; died 640)
**Appearance:** Weathered, warm olive skin and sharp-features. She has dark brown eyes and curly black hair.
**Disposition:** Warm in private, but never soft. Independent, outspoken, and entirely self-directed.
**Current Seat:** Deceased
### About
Seriane was tolerated at court rather than embraced, her heritage granting her authority but not acceptance. She came and went without permission, vanishing for months to wander the frontier and beyond and spending her time with Esra teaching her frontier imperatives rather than palace politeness, instilling strength and self-governance above all else. Esra loved her fiercely but feared becoming her; not unwanted exactly, but still incompatible with the throne. Her death was sudden - a riding accident - and it left Esra with an unrecoverable absence and lack of closure.
