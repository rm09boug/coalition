*Mistress of the Golden Looms; Patroness of the Artisan’s Court*
### Quick Facts
**Birthday:** (Age 42)
**Appearance:** Tall and graceful, with amber-green eyes and chestnut hair streaked with gold thread, her signature mark. Always clad in pale silks of her own making, her presence carries the quiet scent of jasmine and authority.
**Disposition:** Elegant, calculating, and unfailingly composed. Seraline wields charm as deftly as commerce, turning generosity into leverage and silence into profit.
**Current Seat:** The Artisan’s Court, Aurenne City
### About

Seraline Dorr is Aurenne’s sovereign of silk and subtlety, a woman whose voice carries the weight of coin and the softness of charm. Born to a modest family of dyers in Twinriver, she rose through brilliance and calculation, first as a designer of ceremonial garments for Parliament’s Speaker, then as the architect of the Looms’ modern empire.

Under her leadership, the guild transformed from a consortium of tailors into Aurenne’s most powerful cultural syndicate, controlling the dye houses, textile routes, and high fashion of the realm. Nobles wear her fabrics not merely for prestige, but for favor, each thread a quiet bond of allegiance. Seraline understands the politics of texture: a whisper woven into velvet can travel farther than a shout across marble halls.
