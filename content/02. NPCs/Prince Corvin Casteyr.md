*Crown Prince of Aurenne, bearer of the Sigil of Continuance*
### Quick Facts
**Birthday:** 624 (Age 18)
**Appearance:** Tall for his age and quickly developing the stature of a man. Wavy, honey-colored hair, tan skin, and dark green eyes.
**Disposition:** Idealistic, attentive, and tactically intelligent. Earnest but not naive. Developing a temper lately, which he is determined to reign in.
**Current Seat:** His Highness, Crown Prince of Aurenne
### About
Corvin is widely beloved at court and among the people, seen as a natural heir to Roquint’s steadiness but with greater warmth and charisma. He and Esra have a sincere bond rooted in childhood friendship, though their roles have increasingly forced distance between them. Every year he grows taller, brighter, and more beloved - and Esra, watching from the edges, learns to hide her envy behind loyalty. He trusts her, and always will, but he does not entirely understand her anymore.
