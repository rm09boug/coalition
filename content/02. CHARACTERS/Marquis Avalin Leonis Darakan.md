*First Son of [[02. CHARACTERS/Duke Orion Darakan\|Orion Darakan]]; Merchant Apprentice & Right Hand of the [[Maharasa Syndicate]]*
### Quick Facts
**Birthday:** (Age 27)
**Appearance:** Resembling his father, he carries severe brows and a natural downward set to his lips. His eyes, however, are his mother’s: a vivid amethyst. It is rare to catch him smiling.
**Disposition:** Hedonistic, unreliable, quick-witted, and bodacious.
**Current Seat:** 
### About

Overworked and burnt-out, Avalin has little patience - and even less interest - for the grand ambitions that drive the Darakan family forward like a horse under the lash. His motivation began to wither shortly after his eighteenth year, following a youth spent in relentless training and study. Being appointed as his father’s right hand within the trade syndicate only pushed him further from the Darakan Estate. These days, he appears only when absolutely required.

Most of his time is spent attending to private dealings far from his family’s reach. Rumor has it he runs a clandestine gambling ring, though no one has yet proven it, and he certainly isn’t volunteering the truth.
