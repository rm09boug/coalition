*Commander of the Crown Keepers*
### Quick Facts
**Birthday:** (Age 57)
**Appearance:** Ser Varyn Elaroth is tall and severe, his silver hair and pale eyes echoes the steel he wears. His armor, the Castellan’s Aegis, is polished argent-gold plate inscribed with a sunburst. His visor, the Mirror of Oath, is a seamless, featureless mask that erases all expression, his voice within it cold and resonant. At his hip rests Diadrist, a longsword of mithril whose fuller bears a line of gold 
**Disposition:** Severe, composed, and exacting; a man of measured tone whose silences speak louder than reprimands.
**Current Seat:** Diadem Hall, the inner sanctum of the Citadel, Aurenne City
### About

Sir Varyn Elaroth is the immovable pillar of the monarchy’s defense, a man whose calm is more unsettling than fury. Clad always in burnished plate engraved with the sunburst sigil of Aurenne, he is known for his unyielding composure and almost monastic discipline. Once a decorated knight of the Royal Wardens, he was elevated to Knight-General after quelling a mutiny within the Bulwark without drawing his sword.

Varyn believes loyalty is not a matter of heart but of design: a structure to be built, reinforced, and guarded like the walls of the palace itself. Under his command, the Crown Keepers have grown more than ceremonial, they have become the living architecture of royal sanctity. Every hallway they patrol, every chamber they guard, is governed by ritual, timing, and order.

His reputation is both revered and feared. The Keepers muse that he never sleeps within the same chamber twice, for fear that complacency might find him. Others claim his sword, Diadrist, has not been drawn in twenty years, for it is sworn to unsheathe itself only upon royal command.

In Parliament, his name is invoked as a warning:
*“If Resolve leaves the Citadel, the realm is no longer safe, or already lost.”*
