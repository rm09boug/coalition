*Of Blood and Tradition*

Beyond its great provinces and coastal dominions, [[Aurenne]]’s heartland is dotted with dozens of duchies, baronies, and holdfasts, minor realms that trace their lineages to the kingdom’s founding wars. Some rule fertile valleys and riverlands; others command highland fortresses or mist-wrapped moors. Each swears fealty to the Crown but governs with fierce independence, their banners and customs as varied as the soil beneath them. These duchies serve as Aurenne’s lifeblood, managing trade routes, training levies, and preserving the old rites forgotten by the capital. From the marble workshops of [[Aurenne's Heartland#Valmere]] to the storm-worn bastions of [[Aurenne's Heartland#Korrin Peak]], from the plains surrounding [[Aurenne's Heartland#Tareth]] to the twilight vineyards of [[Aurenne's Heartland#Rhaedun]], they are the quiet architecture of the realm’s endurance, a thousand loyal voices upholding the weight of Aurenne.
### Cities

#### Galdmere
*The Forge in the Foothills*
Nestled in the foothills of [[Alar's Fist]], Galdmere is a sturdy, disciplined city where silver and iron are smelted and shaped into coin, jewelry, and weaponry. Guilds hold greater sway than nobles here; to the folks of Galdmere, wealth is earned through hard work, not inheritance.
#### Korrin Peak
*The Crown of the Spine*
High in the mist-wrapped peaks of the northern Spine, Korrin Peak stands as a testament to unity wrought in iron and stone. Once an isolated dwarven hold famed for its deep-forges and echoing halls, it joined the human cities of Aurenne during the [[Pact of Stone and Silver]], when the mountain’s ore veins began to dwindle and trade became lifeblood. Today, its granite terraces ring with the rhythm of hammers and the murmur of mountain winds, where dwarven masons and human engineers labor side by side.

The city’s heart is the [[Deep Forge]], a molten cathedral tended by the descendants of [[Clan Korrin]], whose craftsmanship adorns the crown and armories of Aurenne alike. Steam-driven lifts carry goods from the mines below to the markets above, where coin and craftsmanship hold equal honor. The people of Korrin Peak prize endurance, precision, and quiet faith in the worth of work; every strike of the hammer, they say, echoes the will of the gods that first shaped stone.
#### Rhaedun
*The Twilight Delta*
Where the River Soren meets the sea, Rhaedun sprawls across lush deltas rich with minerals that grant its many vineyards their unique, highly prized characteristics. Barges from [[The Iron Holds]] arrive daily, and great chain-cranes haul ore and armaments from the river to the sea docks, where trade ships bound for [[Aurenne#The Port of Elaren\|Port Elaren]] wait. 
#### Tareth
*The Hewn Heart*
One of the oldest cities in Aurenne, Tareth was born from the bite of the axe. What began as a frontier logging camp beneath the shadow of the southern woods grew into the realm’s beating heart of timber and trade. The forests that once cloaked the hills have long since vanished, replaced by wind-scoured plains and skeletal groves where only stumps and sawdust remember what stood before.

From its hilltop keeps, the city looks down upon an expanse of mills and wagon-tracks that never sleep. Great sawhouses powered by river wheels grind day and night, feeding lumber north to the shipwrights of Port Elaren and the builders of Aurenne City. Though the land bears the scars of its own making, Tareth’s people wear their legacy as pride, pragmatic, unyielding, and quick to build anew from whatever’s left standing.
#### Terrin's Reach
*The Last Light of the West*
South of Argent Bay, Terrin’s Reach clings to salt-grey cliffs battered by surf. Small and windswept, it began as a watch-fort and grew into a haven for pearl-divers and exiles, each with superstitions more outrageous than the last. Its lighthouse, Aurion's Eye, is the last beacon that ships  out of the bay see before open ocean.
#### Thalenport
*The Gilded Threshold*
On the southern coast of Argent Bay, west of [[Aurenne City]], Thalenport serves as a diplomatic center and a stopover for those traveling to the capital. Warehouses crowd its docks, and gilt-domed guildhalls line the port city's streets. Half its population are transient merchants or envoys, the other half destined to become courtiers to the crown.
#### Valmere
*The Jewel of Lake Mer Aurel*
Valmere sits the coast of the vast inland lake called Mer Aurel, and is the heart of the Aurenne’s inland trade; barges drift down its silvery waters laden with grain, silver ingots, and horse tack bound for [[Aurenne#The Port of Elaren\|Port Elaren]]. The city’s low, marble terraces are built around a sprawling marketplace and an ancient bell tower that rings to announce market tides.
