*Breadbasket and Coinhold of Aurenne*

Rolling plains and river-vales rich with farmland and silver-veined hills. [[The Silver Vale]] is famed for their minted coinage, fine horses, and merchant nobles. Pragmatic and profit-driven, their allegiance often follows the weight of gold more than the pull of honor.
### Cities

#### Galdmere
*The Forge in the Foothills*
Nestled in the foothills of [[Alar's Fist]], Galdmere is a sturdy, disciplined city where silver and iron are smelted and shaped into coin, jewelry, and weaponry. Guilds hold greater sway than nobles here; to the folks of Galdmere, wealth is earned through hard work, not inheritance.
#### Norcliff
*The Northern Gateway*
On the northern coast, where the [[River Selmarch]] meets the [[Boreal Sea]], Norcliff stands as the Vale’s northern harbor and its link to the colder, rougher coasts beyond. The city’s high granite walls gleam faintly with embedded ore, and its wharves are crowded with merchant vessels bound inland. Known for shipwrights, fur traders, and the unflinching temperament of its people, Norcliff is weathered, loyal to its contracts, and proud of its endurance.
#### Thalenport
*The Gilded Threshold*
On the southern coast of Argent Bay, west of [[Aurenne City]], Thalenport serves as a diplomatic center and a stopover for those traveling to the capital. Warehouses crowd its docks, and gilt-domed guildhalls line the port city's streets. Half its population are transient merchants or envoys, the other half destined to become courtiers to the crown.
#### Velisere
*The Jewel of Lake Mer Aurel*
Velisere sits the coast of the vast inland lake called Mer Aurel, and is the heart of the Vale’s inland trade; barges drift down its silvery waters laden with grain, silver ingots, and horse tack bound for [[Aurenne#The Port of Elaren\|Port Elaren]]. The city’s low, whitewashed terraces are built around a glittering mint and an ancient bell tower that rings to announce market tides. Bureaucratic and elegant, Velisare is known for contracts, coinage, and calligraphy. Many noble houses trace their fortunes to Velisere’s counting halls.
