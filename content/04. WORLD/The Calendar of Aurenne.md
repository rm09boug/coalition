**Structure:** 12 months of 30 days, 7-day weeks (Rise, Blaze, Balance, Trial, Dusk, Rest, Renewal), plus 5 holy intercalary days — The Days of Balance — totaling 365 days.
 **Current Year:** 642 A.F. (Fifth Great Radiant Cycle — the Cycle of Reflection)
### The Threefold Cycle of Years
Time turns upon the Three Divine Cycles of the Radiant Path — each 300 years — the rhythm of Fall, Balance, and Rise. Together they form the Great Radiant Cycle, nine centuries in which the world is broken, balanced, and reborn anew.

**Anno Fracturis (A.Fr.) – Year of the Broken Flame**
*Era of Kaerath, the Duskwarden.*
The universe began in ruin and fire; each Fracturis marks an age of trial and transformation when the world is tested and remade.

**Anno Aequitas (A.A.) – Year of the Divine Balance**
*Era of Serathiel, Keeper of the Balance.*
When ruin settles, order rises. Law and faith hold creation steady as gods and mortals align.

**Anno Fulgoris (A.F.) – Year of the Radiant Flame**
*Era of Aurion, the Dawnfather.*
From balance, light ascends. Civilization flourishes beneath the Flame’s brilliance. (Current year: 642 A.F.) 

Priests and astrologers often mark Great Radiant Cycles using symbols or constellations tied to each deity:

**Format:**
(Year ###) (Cycle Abbrev.), (Great Cycle Number)

**Example:**
642 A.F., 5th Great Radiant Cycle (The 642nd Year of the Radiant Flame, Fifth Great Cycle)
So the world began in 1 A.Fr., 1st Cycle — “The First Light Born of Shadow.” After every 900 years, the cycle counter increments. Temples and scholars might phrase it as: “Now we dwell in the Fifth Turning of the Flame” or “The Fifth Cycle of the Triad”
### Months of the Year
#### Beginning – Dawntide
**Dawntide** – The awakening of the year; light stirs, oaths are renewed, and the first warmth of Aurion’s blessing returns.
#### Season of Plenty (Aurion — Rise / Renewal)
**Sunspire** – The dawn of planting and promise; life stirs beneath golden fields.
**Verdanspire** – The bloom of faith and fertility; harmony thrives in full color.
**Highspire** – The radiant height of summer; the world basks in divine brilliance.
**Goldspire** – The crown of harvest; gratitude and plenty fill the realm.
#### Season of Transition (Serathiel — Balance / Reflection)
**Umberveil** – The month of order and restoration; laws, justice, and peace reaffirmed.
**Hollowveil** – The waning of light; remembrance, meditation, and preparation for hardship.
#### Season of Scarcity (Kaerath — Fall / Trial)
**Frostfall** – The first chill; endurance and patience temper the faithful.
**Emberfall** – The fires of mourning and resolve; strength forged through hardship.
**Cinderfall** – Vigil and perseverance; warmth held close against deepening dark.
**Ashfall**  – The final test of survival; pain and endurance before the year’s surrender.
#### End – Dusktide
**Dusktide** – The quiet fading of the cycle; reflection, atonement, and stillness before renewal.
### Days of Balance 
The sacred bridge between years. Five holy days of fasting, reflection, and renewal of the Crown’s oath.

- **Day of Flame** – Honors [[Aurion, the Dawnfather]]. The [[Eternal Flame]] is rekindled across temples; candles burn from dusk to dawn to symbolize the renewal of light and life.
- **Day of Scales** – Honors [[Serathiel, the Keeper of Balance]]. Disputes are settled, debts forgiven, and vows measured; the faithful reflect on the fairness of their deeds.
- **Day of Shadow** – Honors [[Kaerath, the Duskwarden]]. A day of solemn silence and humility; lanterns are dimmed, and prayers are whispered for strength through adversity.
- **Day of Reflection** – Dedicated to mortal introspection. Citizens fast and write their sins or regrets upon parchment before casting them into purifying flame or flowing water.
- **Day of Binding Light** – The year’s final rite. The Crown renews its oath before the people, and families share a final feast to mark unity, balance, and the dawn of a new year.
