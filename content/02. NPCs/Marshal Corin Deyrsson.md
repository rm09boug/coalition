*Commander of the Royal Wardens; Lord of Deyrholm Frontier*
### Quick Facts
**Birthday:** (Age late-40s)
**Appearance:** Broad and battleworn, with the heavy shoulders of a lifetime soldier. His left arm bears a half-healed burn scar, a relic from the Siege of Vareth’s Gate. Keeps his armor immaculate, his cloak black with the crimson sigil of the Wardens. Rarely seen without his sword, Vigil’s Oath, a family relic reforged after the rebellion.
**Disposition:** Resolute, proud, fiercely loyal.
**Current Seat:** Deyrholm Frontier.
### About

A towering figure of hard muscle and harder conviction, Marshal Corin Deyrsson is the embodiment of Aurenne’s frontier steel. His weathered face is a map of old scars, each one earned in the skirmishes and sieges that marked his long service along the borderlands. Dark hair shot through with iron-grey frames a stern visage, his deep-set eyes forever measuring threat and terrain alike. Descended from the disgraced Margrave Deyr, once a rebel against the Crown, Corin has spent his life in tireless penance, wielding discipline as both shield and weapon. His loyalty is absolute, his justice severe, his men devoted more from fear than affection.
