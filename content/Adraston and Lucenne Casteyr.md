*Their Highnesses, the Twin Heirs of the Second Queen*
### Quick Facts
**Birthday:** 633 (Age 9)
**Appearance:** Both children have their mother's pale hair and complexion, with their father's severe eyes. Luckily they did not inherit his nose.
**Disposition:** Adraston is impulsive and bright, Lucenne watchful and strangely self-contained. Both are spoiled, but never cruel.
**Current Seat:** Their Highnesses, the Twin Heirs of the Second Queen
### About
The twins are adored by the court; symbols of renewal after tragedy, living assurances that the Casteyr bloodline still blooms. Esra, only twelve years older, has spent much of her life as their caretaker during Sylveth’s absences. They love her fiercely, though in the careless way of children who cannot fathom politics. In them, Esra sees both her greatest tenderness and her most painful reminder: that her father’s love can always start again somewhere else.
